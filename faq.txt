About us

We are ClickBites. We work only with small cafes in India that list their food on Zomato, Swiggy and Instagram.

Most of these cafes have good ratings but almost no photos of the food itself. A customer scrolls, sees a logo or a blurry picture of a plate, and moves on. That costs you orders.

We fix exactly that. We look at your menu, find every dish with no proper photo, shoot a clean photo for each one, then build a poster and a short video ad around it for Zomato, Swiggy and Instagram. We also leave a small tracker on your account so no dish ever goes up without a photo.

We charge Rs 15,000 to set it up, and Rs 3,000 every month to keep it running.

Frequently asked questions

1. What exactly do I get?
You get a photo for every dish on your menu, a poster for each dish built for Zomato and Swiggy, a short video ad for Instagram, and a tracker that flags any dish that is missing a photo.

2. How much does it cost?
Rs 15,000 one-time setup fee, plus Rs 3,000 per month. There is no hidden charge.

3. What does the Rs 15,000 cover?
It covers the first photo shoot, all the posters, the first set of video ads, and installing the tracker on your Zomato, Swiggy and Instagram accounts.

4. What does the Rs 3,000 a month cover?
It covers new dishes you add, updated photos when the dish changes, new posters and videos as needed, and the tracker running on your accounts.

5. How long does the first setup take?
About 7 to 10 days from the day we start. The photo shoot itself takes one afternoon at your cafe.

6. Do I need to close the cafe for the shoot?
No. We work around your hours. We need about two hours with the cafe empty or quiet so the food photographs well.

7. Do you come to my cafe or do I send you the food?
We come to you. We bring our own lights and camera. You just need to cook the dishes as normal.

8. What if my menu has 40 dishes?
We photograph all of them. The Rs 15,000 covers the full menu, however many dishes it has.

9. Do you handle Zomato, Swiggy and Instagram, or only one?
All three. We upload the photos and posters to Zomato and Swiggy, and post the video ads on your Instagram.

10. What kind of video ads do you make?
Short videos, 15 to 30 seconds, showing the dish being made or served. No acting, no fancy edits. Just your food looking good.

11. Will the photos look like my cafe or like a stock photo?
They will look like your cafe. We shoot on your counter, with your plates, in your light. We do not use stock images.

12. Do I keep the photos after I pay?
Yes. Every photo, poster and video belongs to you. You can use them anywhere.

13. What is the tracker and how does it help?
The tracker checks your Zomato and Swiggy menu once a week. If a dish has no photo or a weak one, it flags it and we fix it before a customer notices.

14. What if I add a new dish next month?
Tell us. We photograph it, build a poster and a video for it, and upload it. It is covered by the Rs 3,000 monthly fee.

15. What if a dish changes, like a new recipe?
Same as above. We reshoot, rebuild the poster and video, and update your accounts.

16. How do I get started?
Send us your cafe name, your Zomato and Swiggy links, and a list of your menu. We will look at what is missing and come back with a plan within one working day.

17. What is your contact number?

18. What is your email?
hello@clickbites.in. We usually reply the same day.

19. Which cities do you serve?
We serve cafes in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune and Chennai. For any other city, tell us and we will check.

20. What happens after I pay the Rs 15,000?
We schedule the shoot within two days, complete the full setup within 7 to 10 days, hand over all the photos and videos to you, and switch on the tracker. The monthly service starts from the day the tracker goes live.
